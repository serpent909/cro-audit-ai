What these metrics measure — Google Lighthouse scores every page across five dimensions: Performance (raw speed), Accessibility, Best Practices, SEO, and Progressive Web App. The scores below focus on Performance and its Core Web Vitals — the specific signals Google uses as ranking factors and that directly determine whether visitors stay or leave.

Why it matters for conversion — Google research shows a 1-second increase in load time reduces conversions by up to 7% and increases bounce rate by 32%. Pages scoring below 50 typically lose a significant portion of visitors before they see any content. Core Web Vitals are also a confirmed Google Search ranking signal — poor scores harm organic traffic as well as on-site conversion.

LCP (how fast the main content appears) drives first impressions — above 4s most visitors assume the page is broken. CLS (layout shifts) causes mis-clicks on CTAs as buttons jump while the page loads. TBT / INP measure how responsive the page feels to clicks and form inputs — critical for sign-up and checkout flows. TTFB is the server’s response time and is the foundation every other metric builds on.

    Evidence: Homepage header shows “Interactive Demo” + “Login” and hero CTAs show “Get Started” + “Interactive Demo”; homepage text context includes mailto:...subject=30 Day Free Trial with GreenHalo.

  • Biggest trust/credibility gap: No customer logos, reviews, or case studies detected in text context; only an awards-style image appears. For a data-heavy ESG/carbon platform, missing trust proof (security/compliance/integrations) will likely suppress demo/trial starts.
    Evidence: SOCIAL PROOF: “None detected” on homepage/pricing/about/resourcehub; TRUST SIGNALS include an image alt: “nz project management conference & awards1.png”.

  • Biggest messaging/positioning gap: Above the fold, value prop is directionally clear (“Take the guesswork out of calculating your carbon footprint”), but differentiation vs other carbon accounting tools is not explicit in the first impression (e.g., what’s uniquely automated, what standards, what exports besides Toitū).
    Evidence: Homepage hero headline: “Take the guesswork out of calculating your carbon footprint.” Subhead references “automatic draw…”, but no concrete differentiator statement is visible above the fold beyond that.

    Evidence (PageSpeed): Mobile LCP 4.07s (Poor >4s), Mobile FCP (First Contentful Paint) 3.16s (Poor >3s).

    Evidence: Homepage shows two hero CTAs (“Get Started” + “Interactive Demo”). Pricing shows “Get Started.”

  • CTA consistency verdict: Inconsistent. Homepage promotes “Get Started” + “Interactive Demo,” while elsewhere “30 Day Free Trial” appears only via a mailto “Contact Us” CTA in text context; pricing is strictly “Get Started.”

5) Top 7 issues

1) Confusing conversion path: demo vs signup vs “30 Day Free Trial” contact email are not unified

  • Impact: High
  • Effort: S
  • Confidence: High
  • Evidence:
  • Homepage hero shows two CTAs: “Get Started” and “Interactive Demo” (hero area).
  • Text context CTAs include Contact Us -> mailto:info@greenhalo.nz?subject=30 Day Free Trial with GreenHalo.
  • Pricing page uses “Get Started” only (pricing cards).
  • Recommendation: Choose a primary path per ICP:
  • For mid-market/enterprise: make “Book a demo” (or “Interactive Demo”) primary, with “Start trial” secondary.
  • For self-serve SMB: make “Start free trial” primary, with “View interactive demo” secondary.
    Add one line under CTAs explaining the difference (e.g., “Interactive demo: no setup. Start trial: connect your data.”).
  • Test idea: Hypothesis → A clarified primary CTA + decision microcopy increases demo/trial starts.
  • Primary metric: Demo starts (clicks to /auth/demo) + signup starts (clicks to /auth/signup)
  • Guardrail: Contact form submissions (don’t cannibalize if valuable)
  • MDE (Minimum Detectable Effect): +10–15% increase in combined demo+signup starts

  • Customer logos row (if available), 1–2 quantified outcomes (e.g., time saved, audit readiness), and 1 testimonial with name/company (if permissible).

  • Test idea:
  • Hypothesis: Adding recognizable proof near CTA increases CTA clicks.
  • Primary metric: Hero CTA CTR (Click-Through Rate) to demo/signup
  • Guardrail: Bounce rate
  • MDE: +8–12% CTR lift
